About

Hi! I am Shao Liu. I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at Beijing Institute of Technology. My postdoctoral mentors are Rongchan Zhu and Guopeng Li.

My research interests lie in nonlinear partial differential equations, probability theory, and harmonic analysis, with a focus on deterministic and probabilistic aspects of dispersive equations.

I received my PhD from the University of Bonn in 2026 under the supervision of Herbert Koch and Tadahiro Oh. Here is my short CV.
